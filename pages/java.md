## KONSENS

Since July 2012 I am the chief architect of a backend component of KONSENS, the joint software programme of the German federal and state tax administrations: data management, business processes, and communication with a large number of external systems. I own the architecture and design specifications, coach a team of 15+ engineers, and keep the platform evolving — JBoss EAP, Hibernate, SOAP integrations, Eclipse RCP and Vaadin clients, Maven and Gradle, test-driven throughout, with the environment described in Puppet.

## Before that

Two statutory accident insurance projects (a 40-person new development as team lead for persistence and base components; then further development and large-volume tuning), a JMS-based workflow system on WebLogic, a meter-data acquisition platform polling a large fleet of electricity meters, a Java EE batch framework with guaranteed data integrity under unstable infrastructure, and a test-management application — all between 2007 and 2012, all Java EE.

## What I do for clients

- Architecture and design for Java EE / Jakarta EE platforms — new builds and the ones that need a steady hand.
- JBoss EAP / WildFly migrations and performance work.
- Coaching and reviews for teams; technical leadership on long-running programmes.
- Infrastructure around the platform: containers, Kubernetes, Puppet.
